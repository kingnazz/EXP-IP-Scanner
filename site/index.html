Questions

Simple on purpose

Does it upload scan results?

No. Scans run on your computer. Results are not uploaded, and there is no account, telemetry, or analytics.

How does it know my public IP?

It asks a plain-text lookup service what address you appear as. Like any HTTPS request, that service sees your public address, but the app sends no identifier, scan results, or discovered-device data. You can turn the lookup off in Settings. See the privacy notice.

Does it need administrator rights?

No for normal scanning. The portable edition can be extracted and run without installation or elevation.

Is this a vulnerability scanner?

No. It performs read-only discovery. It does not test passwords, attempt exploits, or make vulnerability claims.

Why no inventory history?

Because this is a field scanner, not an inventory system. One environment’s device list should not follow you into the next job.
