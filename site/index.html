You’ve got questions, we have answers

Built to be simple on purpose

Does it upload scan results?

No. Scanning happens on your computer, and scan results are not uploaded. There is no account, telemetry, or analytics.

Does it need administrator rights?

No for normal scanning. The portable edition can be extracted and run without installation.

Is this a vulnerability scanner?

No. It is a read-only discovery and administration utility. It does not test credentials or exploit devices.

Why no inventory history?

The tool is designed for technicians moving between unrelated environments. One customer’s scan should not follow you into the next visit.
